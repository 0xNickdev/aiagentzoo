ZOOAI X-Ray · browser extension

Privacy policy.
Short, because there is little to tell.

The extension shows the X-Ray of a Solana token: who launched it, which wallets bought in its first seconds, who paid for the launcher. It does not ask who you are, and it has no account or sign-in.

What it reads

  • On the trading sites it works on (pump.fun, DexScreener, gmgn, Axiom, Photon, BullX, Birdeye, Solscan, Jupiter), the address of the page, to find the token address in it.
  • On X (x.com, twitter.com), the text of posts on the screen, to find token addresses in them.
  • Text you select, only when you choose "X-Ray this token" from the right-click menu.
  • The address of the current tab, only when you click the extension's icon.

It does not read anything else on those pages: no wallets, no balances, no forms, no passwords, no messages.

What it sends

  • Token addresses, and nothing else, to the ZOOAI chain reader (north-production-3f77.up.railway.app), which answers with public on-chain facts about that token.
  • When a page shows a trading pool instead of a token, the pool address to DexScreener's public API (api.dexscreener.com) to learn which token it is.

These requests carry no identifier of you. Like any web request, they reach the server from your internet address; the chain reader uses it only to apply its free rate limit and does not store it with what you looked at.

What it keeps

On your own computer only, in the browser's extension storage: the last tokens you opened, the tokens you asked to be alerted about, and where you dragged the on-page pill. Removing the extension removes them. Nothing is synced to us.

What it never does

  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no selling or sharing of data.
  • No access to your wallet, and no transactions: it reads and shows, it never trades.
  • No remote code: everything it runs ships inside the extension.

Permissions, and why

  • activeTab: read the current tab's address when you click the icon.
  • storage: remember your recent tokens, alerts and the pill's position.
  • contextMenus: the "X-Ray this token" right-click item.
  • notifications and alarms: tell you when a token you watch has its dev sell, its pool drained or it graduates, checked once a minute.
  • Access to the trading sites and X listed above: show the verdict on those pages.
